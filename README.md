# Wait for Yay! ✨

A cute and gentle countdown timer for all the good things, big or small! Track moments of joy and look forward to yay moments.

## About

Wait for Yay! helps you visualize the time remaining until pleasant events, turning anticipation into a positive experience. Instead of just counting down to big deadlines, track small joys like "Pizza Night!", "New episode of my show", or "Weekend coffee date".

It's perfect for anyone who loves gentle reminders, needs a little boost, or wants to focus on small joys. Especially helpful for anxious users who benefit from positive anticipation.

### Features

* **Custom Countdowns:** Add timers for any event with a specific date and time.
* **Visual Timer:** See time remaining in days, hours, minutes, and seconds, updating live.
* **Cute Interface:** A colorful and bubbly design to bring a smile. (Currently features cycling background colors!)
* **Emoji Suggestions:** Automatically adds relevant emojis based on event names (e.g., 🍕 for pizza!).
* **Gentle Reminders:** Subtle visual cues highlight events happening soon (within 24 hours).
* **Persistent Storage:** Your countdowns are saved locally within the app.
* **Desktop App:** Runs as a standalone application on your computer.
